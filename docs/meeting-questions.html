Questions for the meeting

Media Masters: wallet, proof of airing, disputes, media house payouts and booking rules

Current what the system does now (it can be changed after the meeting)   Open still to be decided

A. Paying for bookings (the wallet)

  1. 1Should every advertiser pay from a wallet they fund in advance, rather than paying at checkout?
    Current Yes, the wallet is the only way to pay.
  2. 2Who in a company should be allowed to fund the wallet and spend from it?
    Current Only the company owner (team logins removed).
  3. 3When an advertiser books, should the money be held until the spot airs, rather than taken straight away?
    Current Yes, held until the spot is confirmed aired.
  4. 4If an advertiser doesn't have enough in their wallet, should the system stop the booking and ask them to top up?
    Current Yes, with an alert showing how much is missing.
  5. 5Should advertisers get an alert when their balance runs low? Who sets the amount?
    Current Yes, the advertiser sets the amount.
  6. 6Which ways should advertisers be able to fund their wallet: card, bank transfer, USSD?
    Current Test mode now; Paystack later.

B. Proving a spot aired

  1. 7What proof should a media house provide to show a spot aired?
    Current Both the TX log and a clip.
  2. 8How long after a spot's time slot should the media house have to confirm it aired, before admin is alerted?
    Current 12 hours.
  3. 9If a media house couldn't air a spot, should the advertiser get their money back automatically and be offered a reschedule?
    Current Yes.
  4. 10In future, would you consider an independent broadcast-monitoring service to confirm airing automatically?
    Open

C. Disputes

  1. 11After a spot is confirmed aired, how long should the advertiser have to raise a problem?
    Current 18 hours.
  2. 12If nobody disputes within that time, should the money be released to the media house automatically?
    Current Yes.
  3. 13Should the advertiser be able to confirm early, so the media house is paid straight away?
    Current Yes.
  4. 14Which problems count as a valid dispute: not aired, wrong advert, wrong time or programme, cut short? Anything else?
    Open
  5. 15Who decides a dispute, and how fast should it be resolved?
    Current A Media Masters admin.
  6. 16Can a dispute end in a partial refund, for example a 60-second spot cut to 30 seconds?
    Open

D. Media house money

  1. 17Should media houses see booked but not-yet-aired spots as "Expected", and only receive the money as "Earned" after airing?
    Current Yes.
  2. 18Does Media Masters keep a commission on each spot? What percentage?
    Open To be decided; built as a setting starting at 0%.
  3. 19How should media houses be paid: on request, or automatically (weekly, monthly)?
    Open
  4. 20Until online payouts are connected, is it acceptable for admin to send payouts by bank transfer and mark them paid in the system?
    Open
  5. 21Is there a minimum amount before a media house can request a payout?
    Open

E. Booking rules

  1. 22Should an advertiser be limited to one spot length per programme or time belt per day (e.g. 60s or 30s in News at 10, not both)?
    Current Yes.
  2. 23Can an advertiser book several spots of the same length in the same programme on the same day?
    Current Yes.
  3. 24Should the campaign's air date decide the booking date, with the media house confirming or asking to reschedule?
    Current Yes.
  4. 25Is 7.5% VAT always added on top of the rate card price?
    Current Yes.
  5. 26Should booking 3 or more media houses in one checkout earn a discount? How much?
    Current 5% for 3+ media houses, 10% for 5+.

F. Accounts

  1. 27Should each company have just one login (the owner)? Or do you want team members back later, with limits on what they can do?
    Current One login; the team feature is switched off but can be restored.
  2. 28For media houses, who should have the login: one person, or several staff?
    Open
